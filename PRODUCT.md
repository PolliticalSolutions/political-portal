# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

The public Political Solutions site serves Conservative association officers, agents, candidates,
campaign managers, councillors, council groups, MPs' offices and other campaign professionals who
are evaluating campaign support, constituency intelligence, marked-register processing or an
association subscription. They understand political and campaign terminology and need concise,
credible information before enquiring, subscribing or signing in.

## Product Purpose

The public site presents Political Solutions' data-led campaign management and consultancy offer,
explains the distinct supporting data capabilities and routes visitors to an appropriate next step.
It must establish professional credibility, make commercial and operational boundaries clear, and
help visitors enquire or subscribe without unsupported promises. The protected portal is a separate
operational product surface and is outside the default public-site register.

## Brand Personality

Authoritative. Direct. Grounded.

Political Solutions should feel trusted, professional, sharp and modern. It should communicate like
an experienced campaign professional: confident without shouting, precise without over-explaining,
and politically informed without resembling party communications.

## Anti-references

The public site must not resemble a campaign leaflet, party website, political press release,
generic SaaS template, startup pitch deck, news site or government department. Avoid overt party
branding, politically garish red-white-and-blue treatments, purple gradients, decorative gold,
stock-politician or people-at-laptops imagery, bubbly cards, inflated claims, competing calls to
action and gratuitous animation.

## Design Principles

1. Lead with the campaign job and the decision the visitor needs to make.
2. Earn credibility through precise evidence, prices, boundaries and next steps rather than claims of superiority.
3. Keep campaign support, Constituency Intelligence and Marked Register Processing distinct even when they work together.
4. Make the primary action unmistakable and explain failure, empty and validation states in actionable language.
5. Preserve the established Political Solutions identity and keep the public brand surface visually separate from the protected portal.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Maintain keyboard access, visible focus, semantic heading and form structure,
programmatically associated labels and errors, sufficient colour contrast, reduced-motion support,
clear status announcements, descriptive link and button labels, and usable layouts at desktop and
mobile widths. Never rely on colour alone to communicate status or required action.
