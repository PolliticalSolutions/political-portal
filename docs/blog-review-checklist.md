# Blog Review Checklist

Decision options: `Publish` / `Revise` / `Discard`

## Content quality

- Does the article open with the problem or stakes, rather than a definition or rhetorical question?
- Is there at least one thing the reader can do differently after reading it?
- Is the tone plain-spoken and authoritative rather than corporate, academic, or salesy?
- Has any padding, filler, or throat-clearing been cut?
- Are the subheadings informative rather than vague?
- Is the CTA specific and earned?

## Factual and editorial

- Are all factual claims supported by cited sources?
- Are the sources credible and authoritative?
- Does anything read as fabricated, overclaimed, or legally risky?
- Are the inline citations sufficient for each substantive paragraph?
- Does the `Sources` section match the machine-readable source bundle?

## Commercial standard

- Is this genuinely useful to a campaign professional?
- Would this build trust in Political Solutions as an authority?
- Is there any product pushing that crosses the line from useful to salesy?
