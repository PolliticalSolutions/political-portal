# Build a resilient campaign data baseline

Campaign operations break down when teams treat data hygiene as a one-off task.

## What to standardise first

- Agree ownership for source datasets
- Define a weekly validation cadence
- Track exception handling and turnaround time

## Operational checklist

1. Validate incoming files against agreed schema
2. Reconcile missing records before exports
3. Log changes for auditability
