# Improve campaign handoffs from planning to field delivery

Handoffs are a frequent failure point in campaign operations.

## Where teams lose momentum

- Briefs are not scoped to delivery constraints
- Ownership is unclear between analysis and field teams
- Status updates are inconsistent across channels

## A tighter handoff approach

1. Use one briefing template for every delivery run
2. Confirm acceptance criteria before execution
3. Run a short retrospective after each cycle
